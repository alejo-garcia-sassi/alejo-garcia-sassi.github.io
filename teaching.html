Teaching & Supervision

To understand something is to make it intuitive; to teach it is to offer simple paths that make it so. Whatever the level, what I care about most is the bond between teacher and student — reading, in real time, what a group has and hasn't understood, and building the small models and images that turn an apparently difficult idea into an intuitive one. I put particular effort into clear exposition and into treating the blackboard as a narrative object, so that a course reads as a single connected story rather than a list of results.
